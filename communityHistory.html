HISTORY

About 108 years ago, Isaac Kachada Chiumbu was called by God to expand the Mission of the Kingdom of Heaven in Southern and Central Africa. Reverend Samson Sibanda Kantini and his family starting with his wife, Prophetess Sophia Mandhiyamba, about 104 years ago received their commission under this calling and leadership of Prophet IK Chiumbu to lead the expansion of the Mission north of the Zambezi River in the present-day Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique and the Democratic Republic of Congo. The Mission was founded and grew, like the Biblical early church, from Zimbabwe and Zambia to the rest of the Southern and Central African region. It is the case of what may be called an African endogenous church that evolved outside the imperialist Christian World Mission and Evangelism stage. This is because its evolution is rooted in the outpouring of the Holy Spirit on an individual, Isaac Kachada Chiumbu, while working underground at Etna mine in Zimbabwe without any human mediation but a face-to-face encounter with God alone like Moses before the burning thorn bush or Saul on the road to Damascus. The gospel that Isaac Chiumbu proclaimed was received as ultright rebellion against Western Christian perspectives, imperialism, and African occultic practices. He established a Mission that adopted and followed Biblical methods of the recorded experiences of the early church in the Book of the Acts of the Apostles yet beginning from Genesis.

The Mission took root and developed on its own: first within Zimbabwe where it was born out of the pioneering work of Prophet IK Chiumbu with his young brother, Reverend Levy Savvya Nyirenda Nyangamire, and second in Zambia where it expanded with the commissioning and missionary work of the family of Reverend Samson Sibanda Kantini, and then spread beyond these two countries to the Southern, Central and Eastern African regions.
