ACTIVITIES

The APCH Community involves believers in knowledge sharing and faith-building activities that mainly include but not limited to the following:

Holy Convocation

A holy fellowship of Yahweh with the saints. It involves prayers, the sharing of psalms, and study of the Word of Yahweh. The saints come into Yahweh’s presence in this manner thrice a week on the fourth, fifth and eighth day.
The Eighth or First Day of the week is the Main Divine Service during which the saints come with offerings and tithes as well. This is the day when Yahweh gave light to the dark heavens and earth (Genesis 1:3) and separated light from the darkness (Genesis 1:4). It is on this day that the redemption of the Israelites from their bondage which mirrors the redemption of the whole world through Yehoshua HaMeshaich happened (John 3:16; Exodus 12:16). On this day, the LORD Yehoshua HaMeshaich resurrected from the dead and was worshipped (Matthew 28:16). The first Apostles met, expounded scriptures and broke bread on this first day of the week (Acts 20:7). This day is called the LORD’s Day (Revelations 1:10) and Yahweh instructed that there should always be a holy convocation on the first day of the week (Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7; Numbers 29:1).

Solemn Feasts

Seven days General Conference held thrice a year in April, July and October with neither the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth which is Yehoshua HaMeshiach (1 Corinthians 5:7, 8). The LORD’s Supper is conducted during each Solemn Feast.

Hossanah

Conducted every morning, afternoon and evening of each day of the week to dedicate and give thanks for the day and night to Yahweh. The morning and evening sessions are done by each group of neighbouring households while the afternoon session is conducted by individuals wherever they may be.

Prayer Retreats

Traditionally, prayer retreats are held every Friday and Tuesday. Special retreats are organised on request and directive from Yahweh. These prayers take place at retreat centres called Rushanga, the Sacred Place and/or Holy Mountain.

Bible Study

Indepth systematic exploration of sacred scriptures to build and equip believers with effective and efficient understanding and application of the Word of Yahweh.

Community Outreach

Home visitation conducted by congregation leadership and reaching out to the world with the gospel of the Kingdom of Yahweh and planting churches by the team of evangelists and preachers.

Family

The Apostles Church conducts weekly gatherings, seminars and other activities that inculcate and uphold values and principles from the Biblical Worldview on marriage, parenting and family life through CHINAI (which means the fifth element) Fellowships for the Women, Men, Couples and Youth.

Health and Environment

The Mission promotes diatery and ecological consciousness and faith healing practices for health communities and sustainable environment.

Education

The Mission supports lifelong learning and functional literacy initiatives in congregations. Through ACTS – Atumwi Culture and Theological Seminary – the Mission trains women and men in overseeing, evangelising and managing congregations.

Body Fitness

The Mission organises body fitness activities to promote health living and build such values as perseverance, team work and unity.

Arts and Culture

The Mission helps believers to hone the creative gifts and skills through programmes that serve specific interests in Music and Arts. The Mission network with the Christian community creative come-together festivals and competitions.

charity

The Mission has widow, orphans and vulnerable community services in all congregations. The orphans and widows are integrated in homes of the believers and empowered through the collective wealth generation activities and share out as integral members of the families.
