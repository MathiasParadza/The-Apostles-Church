ABOUT THE APCH

Purpose

The purpose of the Church is to glorify God by obeying the Great Commandment (Matthew 22: 36-40) and the Great Commission (Matthew 28: 18-20), to minister to the needs of the members and non-members as the Church may be able to do so and to do any and all things related to and in connection with the carrying out of the aim and purpose of the Kingdom of Jehovah God.

Mission

To produce disciples of Jesus Christ involved in the following:

(1) Worship – (Celebrating) “To love God with all your heart…” (Deuteronomy 6:4-5; Matthew 22:36-40);
(2) Ministry – (Caring) “Love your neighbour as yourself…” (Mathew 22:36-40);
(3) Evangelism – (Cultivating) “Go…and make disciples…” (Mathew 28:18-20);
(4) Discipleship – (Communicating) “Teach them to observe…” (Mathew 28:18-20);
(5) Community – (Unity) “That they all may be one; as thou, Father, [art] in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me” (John 17:21).

OBJECTIVES

(1) To proclaim the Gospel of the Kingdom of God by any available means consistent with the teaching of God’s word.
(2) To establish Church congregations having their own leadership.
(3) To provide ministries and services to every Church congregation, which will aid in its development and maturity.
(4) To instruct believers with regard to holiness of life, witnessing, giving and exercise of spiritual gifts.
(5) To provide encouragement and a system of analysis for each Church congregation, that will contribute to a healthy growth rate and sustainability.
(6) To develop a strategy for witness and outreach that will provide opportunity for service and encourage a concern for the unreached in all nations.
(7) To promote functional literacy in the Church so that every believer can read and write both the word and the world.
(8) To finance the total Church ministry through self-supporting Church congregations.
(9) To maintain a balanced concern for the social needs of people in the Church and in the community at large where possible.
(10) To co-operate with other legally recognised groupings whose beliefs, values and practices are compatible with those of the Apostles’ Church Mission.
