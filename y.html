Articles of Faith

The following are the articles of Faith of th Church:

SCRIPTURE
We believe the Holy Bible, both Old and New Testaments, is an inspired Word of Jehovah God, without any error as originally given, the all-sufficient and supreme authority in matters of faith, doctrine and spiritual living (2 Timothy 3:16; Revelations 22:18-20).

GOD
We believe in Jehovah God as the Supreme Divine Creator and one eternal God Jehovah (Genesis 1:1; John 1:1-2; Hebrews 1:1-3; Mathew 28:19-20).

JESUS CHRIST
We believe in Jesus Christ as the eternal son God incarnate. The incarnation includes the divine conception, virgin birth and true humanity of Jesus Christ, yet without sin. His death was a voluntary substitutionary atonement. His resurrection and ascension were bodily, as his return will be: “This same Jesus” will come again, personally and visibly. Meanwhile he sits at the right hand of the Father and is now our High Priest and Advocate (Mathews 1:21-23; Mark 16:19; Luke 1:25, John 1:1, 14:18, 10:17-18,37-38, 19:1-42, 20:27-29; 1 John 2:1-2, Isaiah 9:6, 53:1-5,12; Revelation 1:7-8; Psalms 110:1, Hebrews 4:14-15, 8:6).

THE HOLY SPIRIT
We believe in the Holy Spirit as the power of Jehovah God who convicts men of sin, regenerates them, baptizing them into the body of Jesus Christ at their conversion, seals, indwells, sanctifies and fills believers, producing in them the fruits of the Spirit and power for service (John 16:7-11, 3:5-7, 1 Corinthians 12:12-13, Ephesians 1:13-14, 4:30, John 15:5, Galatians 5:22-23, Acts 1:8, 1 Corinthians 12:4-11, Ephesians 4:11-12, Acts 15:14, 10:44-45, 5:19-20, 9:4-7, 10:10-17, 11:5-10, 12:7-11, 13:2, 18:9-10, 21:4, 9-11, 22:6-11, 17:21, 23:11, 26:14-18, 27:23-25).

LIFE
We believe in the sanctity of life in all its forms (Genesis 1: 26-27; Exodus 20:13; Matthew 19:18; Leviticus 17: 10-14; Deuteronomy 12:23; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Mark 12:31; Psalm 139:13-16, Jeremiah 1:5).

HUMAN
We believe that human was created both male and female in the image of God (Genesis 1:27) but was tempted and fall into sin (Genesis 3; 1 Corinthians 15:22) and therefore became carnal (Roman 5:12-21). Unless he is saved by the grace of Jehovah God through Jesus Christ, human stands eternally condemned (John 3:16).

DEATH
We believe that death does not end life (Luke 8:52; 1 Thessalonians 4:13). Death separates human from God (Genesis 3:23); human from the Holy Spirit (Genesis 6:3); and the human body that returns to dust (Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 30:19) from the breathed spirit (Genesis 2:7) that continues to live (Genesis 4:10-11) and death is the last enemy to be destroyed (1 Corinthians 25:16).

SALVATION
We believe that eternal salvation is the free gift of Jehovah God, entirely apart from man’s works, and is possessed by any and all who have faith in and receive Jesus Christ as their personal Lord and Saviour (Romans 3:20-27). Salvation brings forgiveness of sins: this includes deliverance from the penalty and power of sin and ultimately from the presence of sin.

HOLY LIFE
We believe that having received Jehovah God’s gift of eternal life and become a new creation in Jesus Christ, the believer is called to a life of holiness which is spirituality and good works as taught in the scriptures (Hebrews 10:24-25; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11-14, 3:1,8,14) . Spirituality comes with acknowledgement of one’s fallibility and consciously confessing our sins to repentance and allowing the Holy Spirit to inspire our thoughts and actions so that they remain consistent with the Word of Jehovah God (Galatian 5:16). Spirituality is incomplete without good works (James 2:14-17).

CHURCH
We believe the Church is the Ekklesia of God, the body and Kingdom of Jesus Christ of which Jesus Christ is the Head and King built on the foundation rock of the Apostles (Matthew 16:16-18; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 1:22-23; 1 Peter 2:9-10; 1 Corinthians 3:16,17). We also believe in a regenerated Church membership: that is, the Ekklesia is composed of believers from every tribe, tongue, race and nation, who, of their own free will, understand their need for Jesus Christ and therefore accepted Him as their personal Lord and Saviour and are baptized by the Holy Spirit (John 15:1-8; Mark 16:15-16).

BAPTISM
We believe that baptism is by immersion in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit (i.e. Triune immersion) and that the Lord’s Supper is a memorial to the suffering and death of Jesus Christ on the cross. These are ordinances to be observed by the Church in its present age (Mathew 28:19-20; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26; Mathew 26:26-28).

RESURRECTION
We believe in the bodily resurrection of the living dead, the everlasting blessedness of the saved in heaven, and the everlasting punishment and separation from Jehovah God of the unsaved in hell (1 Corinthians 15:35-44, 1 Corinthians 4:13-18, 2 Corinthians 1:7-10; Revelations 21:8).

CULTURE
We believe that culture everywhere in the world is a sum total of the lived experiences under particular material conditions created by a given social, economic and political order and this plays an indispensable role in the spirituality of believers. Aspects of culture that are consistent with the Word of Jehovah God should be upheld for mutual enrichment and efficiency while those aspects of culture that are inconsistent with the Word of Jehovah God should be rejected accordingly (Romans 12:2; Mark 7:1-12; Acts 15:1-31).

ESCHATOLOGY
We believe that there will be signs and happenings to mark the end times including:
(1) The rapture of the Church, that when Christ returns all true believers shall be caught up to meet him in the air (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17: 1 Corinthians 15:51-52).
(2) The revelation of the man of sin (2 Thessalonians 2:3-9).
(3) The great tribulation period (Mathew 24:21-22; Revelation 7:14).
(4) The judgment of the nations and the appearing of all peoples before the judgment seat of Jesus Christ to account for their deeds and receive their reward (Mathew 25:31-32; 1 Corinthians 3:12-15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:17).
(5) The marriage supper of the Lamb (Revelation 19:7-8).
(6) The return of Jesus Christ with His saints (1 Thessalonians 3:13; Jude 1:14).
(7) The reign of Jesus Christ upon the earth for a thousand years (Revelation 20:11-15).
(8) The final blessedness of all believers forever with the Lord (1 Thessalonians 4:17).
