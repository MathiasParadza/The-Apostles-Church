DELIMITATION

The APCH is organized into four sections

Preaching Point

This is the smallest assembly of the Church operating as a satellite point for the nearest branch. It is any assembly point where the membership is less than forty baptised members headed by an appointed teacher assisted by a deacon, secretary and treasurer elected from among the members of that assembly.

Branch

This is the foundation of APCH constituted by a minimum of forty and maximum of one hundred forty-four (144) baptised members under the leadership of a Pastor. Unless particular circumstances of its membership do not permit, when the membership of the Branch exceeds 144, an evangelist organizes preaching points at the furthest places where some of the members are coming from to ensure efficiency and spirit of community is not compromised.

Region

A Region is defined geographically as configured and recommended by the Secretariat to, and approved by, the Synod Executive. A minimum of 1000 baptised members is required before any Region can be allowed to establish Regional leadership.

The General Conference

The General Conference is the Supreme Body of APCH. There are three (3) General Conferences held every year on a quarterly basis in selected regions in the months of April, July and October (Deuteronomy 16) during which the Lord’s Table is served.
