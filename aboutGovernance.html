GOVERNANCE

The APCH is organised and governed by Councils which are made up of two types of sub-committees responsible for coordinating and synchronizing all areas of the ministry and task force committees under the Council’s jurisdiction. The first type is Ecumenical sub-committee for directing all ecumenical activities including doctrinal teaching and guardianship of the Church. All the clergy belong to ecumenical subcommittees. The second type is Administration sub-committee which oversees all administrative and management functions in the Church. All administration officers belong to administration subcommittees. The clergy qualify to be appointed to administration subcommittees just as administration officers qualify to be appointed to ecumenical subcommittees.

Branch Council

The Branch Council is made up of all leaders of the congregation chaired by the Pastor and deputised by the Evangelist. This council oversees the congregation and accordingly all Branch Subcommittees report to it and its Executive Committee submit a consolidated report to the Regional Council.

Regional Council

The Regional Council is made up of all leaders of the region led by an Executive Committee made up of elected members from among leaders in the region. All Regional Subcommittees report to the Regional Council and its Executive Committee submits a consolidated report to the Synod.

Synod

The Synod is the APCH highest governing council on earth and represent the authority of the General Conference. The Synod consists of the Synod Executive and all Regional Bishops. The Vice General Overseer is the chairperson of the Synod. Its Executive headed by the General Overseer is the Supreme Governing Body of APCH and represents the Synod and the General Conference when they are not in session.
