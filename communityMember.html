COMMUNITY

Membership

Description

Membership in community of the Apostles’ Church consists of all persons who have met the requirements for membership, having been approved by the congregation and are listed on the membership role. There shall be a register for children consecrated and a child can only remain in that register if he or she is below the age of eighteen (18).
Once one has reached the age of eighteen and has not attained membership as per the requirement of Article 5, Section 5.2, he or she shall be removed from the register of children and shall be treated as an ordinary visitor in the Church. Certificates of concentration shall be given to all children and are listed in the register for children just like certificates of baptism shall be given to all baptized persons and are listed in the baptism register.

Qualification For Membership

(1) A personal commitment of faith in Jesus Christ for salvation;
(2) Baptism by triune immersion as a testimony of salvation;
(3) Registered at any church branch of the Apostles’ Church;
(4) Shall have completed foundational lessons of the church’s doctrines including the aims, objects and Statement of Faith of the Apostles’ Church;
(5) Consecration and dedication of a child to Jehovah God;
(6) Adoption from another Church whose basis of faith is in conformity with that of the Apostles’ Church.

Membership By Transfer

(1) From another Church branch of the Apostles’ Church

A member of a congregation of the Apostles’ Church who moves to another assembly’s ministry area can have his membership transferred on presentation of a letter from the congregation of origin. The letter explains how the transferred member has been standing in his or her Spiritual life and whether he or she has been involved in any position of responsibility at the branch and how he or she has been fulfilling his or her duties. The member is then enlisted at the new branch and be removed from the active roll of the branch she or he has left.

(2) From a Church that is in agreement with Apostles’ Church

A person who comes from a Church with which the APCH is in agreement and brings a letter of recommendation saying that he is in good standing and is received at the Lord’s Table may be received into membership of the Apostle’s Church. In addition to this letter, the branch leadership must be satisfied that such a person is walking in fellowship with the Lord and that s/he understands and accepts the aims, objectives and Statement of Faith of the APCH. In some cases, further instructions from the Holy Spirit may be necessary before he is accepted as a member.

(3) From a Church that is not in agreement with Apostles’ Church

A person who comes from a Church whose doctrines are not in full agreement with the Apostles’ Church is baptized in order to be accepted as a member.

Designation of Membership

To properly reflect the membership of the Church, each assembly maintains a membership roll of active and/or resident members who reside within the assembly’s ministry area or are currently active in the Church. Any person who is accepted as a member of a congregation whether by consecration, baptism, adoption or transfer is publicly welcomed as a member at a regular service at that congregation. Each member of the APCH has membership registration in one congregation only.

Members’ Rights and Responsibilities

Consistent with the terms and conditions sanctioned by the APCH Constitution
(1) Every active member has the right to participate in all matters and business of the Church including financial affairs and administration of the Church.
(2) Every member has the responsibility of attending all divine services of worship and the Lord’s Table.
(3) Every member has full voting rights with a responsibility of business meetings with voting rights. Where elections are conducted each member is entitled to one vote. Voting by proxy is strictly prohibited.
(4) Members support and promote the Church through prayers, giving tithes and honouring financial obligations as may be agreed upon by the Church, offering donations and labour as well as community services to the needy.
(5) Taking full advantage of teaching programmes offered by the Church.
(6) All members carry out any other duty assigned to them by the Church authorities at various levels aimed at attainment of the aims and objects of the Holy Bible.
(7) Every member has, subject to the discipline and disciplinary regulations of the Church, responsibility to honour disciplinary action that may be meted out on him or her as the case may be.

Exclusion From Membership

Members are removed from the Church roll for the following reasons:
(1) Gross indiscipline.
(2) Transfer of membership to a different Church.
(3) Apostasy.
(4) Death.

Restoration of Members

Members removed from the Church roll are restored by the vote of the assembly from which the member was previously dismissed or the unanimous agreement of the officers according to the spirit of 2 Corinthians 2:5-11.

Membership Visitation

(1) A member of the APCH who visits another branch is welcomed into full fellowship on presentation of a letter of recommendation from his or her Church branch. A person who returns after an extended period of visit is required bring a letter back to his or her original Church branch indicating the conditions of his walk while on the visit.
(2) A visitor to a Church branch of the APCH who is a member of another Church with which the Apostles’ Church is in agreement is welcomed to fellowship provided s/he brings a letter of recommendation from his or her home Church. This privilege of fellowship does not give such a person voting rights or allow him to hold office in the APCH. Should such a person anticipate an extended stay s/he is encouraged to come into full membership of the Church branch of the APCH.
(3) A visitor to a Church branch of the APCH who is a member of a Church that is not in agreement with the APCH is welcomed in the same manner as a visitor who is not a member of any other Church and participation of such a visitor is limited to attendance of a divine service and any Church activity that is of a public nature.
