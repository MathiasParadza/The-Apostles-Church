ABOUT THE APCH

Affiliation

This Apostles’ Church is autonomous and maintains the right to govern its own affairs, independent of any denominational and/or religious sectarian control. Notwithstanding, the Church retains the right and freedom to associate itself, and terminate such association should by the standards of the Church be found wanting, with movements, councils and associations of Churches different from itself that in the discretion of the Synod such association is considered essential to the furtherance of the aim and objects of the Holy Bible. Currently, the Church is affiliated with the Pentecostal Fellowship of Zambia.
