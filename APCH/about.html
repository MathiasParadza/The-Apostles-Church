About The Apostles' Church

Discover our purpose, mission, and vision as we seek to glorify God and serve our community through faith in Jesus Christ.

Our Purpose

The purpose of The Apostles' Church is to glorify God by obeying the Great Commandment and the Great Commission, to minister to the needs of both members and non-members as we are able, and to carry out all things related to advancing the Kingdom of Jehovah God.

"Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the Law?" Jesus replied: "'Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.' This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: 'Love your neighbor as yourself.' All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments." — Matthew 22:36-40
"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age." — Matthew 28:18-20

Our Mission

To produce disciples of Jesus Christ involved in five essential areas of Christian life and ministry:

Worship (Celebrating)

"To love God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength" (Deuteronomy 6:4-5; Matthew 22:36-40)

Ministry (Caring)

"Love your neighbor as yourself" (Matthew 22:36-40)

Evangelism (Cultivating)

"Go and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:18-20)

Discipleship (Communicating)

"Teach them to observe all that I have commanded you" (Matthew 28:18-20)

Community (Unity)

"That they all may be one... that the world may believe" (John 17:21)

Our Objectives

To fulfill our mission and purpose, we are committed to the following objectives:

  • 1
    To proclaim the Gospel of the Kingdom of God by any available means consistent with the teaching of God's Word.
  • 2
    To establish Church congregations having their own leadership and spiritual authority.
  • 3
    To provide ministries and services to every Church congregation, aiding in its development and spiritual maturity.
  • 4
    To instruct believers regarding holiness of life, witnessing, giving, and the exercise of spiritual gifts.
  • 5
    To provide encouragement and a system of analysis for each Church congregation, contributing to healthy growth and sustainability.
  • 6
    To develop a strategy for witness and outreach that provides opportunities for service and encourages concern for the unreached in all nations.
  • 7
    To promote functional literacy in the Church so that every believer can read and write both the Word and the world.
  • 8
    To finance the total Church ministry through self-supporting Church congregations.
  • 9
    To maintain a balanced concern for the social needs of people in the Church and in the community at large where possible.
  • 10
    To cooperate with other legally recognized Christian organizations whose beliefs, values, and practices are compatible with those of The Apostles' Church.
