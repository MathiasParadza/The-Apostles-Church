Begin Your Journey with Christ

In Seven Transformative Steps

1

Believe in Him

"For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life." — John 3:16

Faith in Jesus is the foundation of your journey. It starts with believing that He is the Son of God who came to save us from sin. His love is unconditional, and His grace is freely given. When you put your trust in Him, you receive the gift of salvation and eternal life. No matter your past, Jesus welcomes you with open arms.

2

Repent and Be Baptized

"Repent, then, and turn to God, so that your sins may be wiped out, that times of refreshing may come from the Lord." — Acts 3:19

Repentance is turning away from sin and choosing to follow Christ. It is not just about feeling sorry for mistakes but making a heart-driven decision to live according to God's will. Baptism is a powerful declaration of your faith, symbolizing the washing away of sin and the start of a new life in Christ. It is a step of obedience, sealing your commitment to Him.

3

Walk in His Word

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path." — Psalm 119:105

God's Word is our guide through life. The Bible teaches, corrects, and encourages us, helping us grow spiritually. Daily prayer and study of the Scriptures strengthen your faith and deepen your relationship with God. When you seek His guidance and live by His Word, you will experience peace, wisdom, and direction in every step you take.

4

Join Our Church Family

"They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer." — Acts 2:42

Faith is strengthened in community. God designed the church to be a family of believers who support, uplift, and encourage one another. Through worship, fellowship, and service, you will find a place where you belong. Being part of a church allows you to grow in faith, discover your spiritual gifts, and experience the joy of walking with fellow believers.

5

Share the Gospel

"Go into all the world and preach the gospel to all creation." — Mark 16:15

Faith is not meant to be kept to yourself. As believers, we are called to spread the good news of Jesus Christ. Share your testimony with friends, family, and those around you. Let your actions reflect God's love and grace, showing others the transformative power of faith through both words and deeds.

6

Serve Others with Love

"Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's grace in its various forms." — 1 Peter 4:10

True discipleship is demonstrated through service. Whether through acts of kindness, volunteering, or ministry, serving others is a powerful way to reflect Christ's love. Find ways to give back within your church and community, using your unique gifts to bless those in need and embody the servant heart of Jesus.

7

Remain Faithful and Endure

"But the one who stands firm to the end will be saved." — Matthew 24:13

The Christian journey is not always easy, but perseverance in faith brings eternal rewards. Stay strong in your walk with Christ through prayer, worship, and continued spiritual growth. Surround yourself with a faith-filled community that encourages and uplifts you, and always trust in God's promises, knowing that He is faithful to complete the work He began in you.

Are you ready to take the next step?

What Is the Coming of Christ?

Understanding biblical prophecy about Christ's return and what it means for believers today.

The Coming of Christ refers to the promised return of Jesus Christ to consummate God's redemptive plan for humanity and creation. The Holy Bible teaches that Jesus, who ascended bodily into heaven, will return personally, visibly, and gloriously—"this same Jesus" (Acts 1:9–11).

The Nature of Christ's Coming

The return of Christ will not be symbolic or spiritual only, but literal and bodily. As He rose from the dead and ascended into heaven, so He will return in power and glory (Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7).

Key Aspects of the Coming of Christ

According to Scripture and the doctrine of The Apostles' Church, the Coming of Christ includes:

  • • The Rapture of the Church – Jesus will return for His Church, and all true believers—both living and dead—will be caught up to meet Him in the air (1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:51–52).
  • • The Great Tribulation – A period of unprecedented suffering and judgment upon the earth, during which the man of sin will be revealed (Matthew 24:21–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–9).
  • • The Judgment of the Nations – All people will appear before the judgment seat of Christ to give account of their lives and receive their eternal reward or condemnation (Matthew 25:31–32; 2 Corinthians 5:10).
  • • The Return of Christ with His Saints – Christ will return to earth with His redeemed people to establish His righteous reign (1 Thessalonians 3:13; Jude 1:14).
  • • The Millennial Reign and Eternal State – Jesus Christ will reign upon the earth, followed by the final judgment and the everlasting blessedness of believers with the Lord (Revelation 20; 1 Thessalonians 4:17).

A Call to Readiness

The Coming of Christ calls believers to live holy, watchful, and faithful lives. It is a message of hope for the righteous and a solemn warning to those who reject God's grace (Matthew 24:42–44; Titus 2:11–13).

Finding Healing and Hope

A compassionate guide to overcoming self-harm through faith, community, and God's healing love.

Healing and Hope in the Gospel

Healing and hope are central to the Gospel of Jesus Christ. The Apostles' Church believes that Jehovah God is both willing and able to bring healing—spiritual, emotional, and physical—according to His divine will and purpose.

God as the Source of Healing

God reveals Himself in Scripture as a compassionate healer: "I am the Lord that healeth thee" (Exodus 15:26). Healing flows from God's love, power, and redemptive work through Jesus Christ, whose atoning sacrifice addressed the brokenness caused by sin (Isaiah 53:4–5; Matthew 8:16–17).

Healing Through Jesus Christ

Jesus' earthly ministry demonstrated God's heart for the suffering. He healed the sick, restored the broken, delivered the oppressed, and brought hope to the hopeless (Luke 4:18–19). Today, Christ continues to heal through:

  • • Prayer and faith – Bringing our burdens to God in sincere prayer and trusting in His power to heal and restore.
  • • The ministry of the Holy Spirit – The Comforter who brings peace, wisdom, and strength in times of distress.
  • • The support and care of the Church community – Walking alongside one another in love, bearing each other's burdens.

Spiritual Healing and Salvation

The greatest healing is salvation, the restoration of humanity's broken relationship with God. Through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ, individuals receive forgiveness, new life, and eternal hope (Romans 5:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17).

Hope in Every Circumstance

Our hope as Children of God is not based on circumstances but on God's unchanging promises. Even in suffering, believers are assured that God is present, working all things for good, and leading them toward eternal glory (Romans 8:18, 28; Revelation 21:4).

The Church's Role in Healing

The Church is called to walk alongside those in pain, offering prayer, compassion, practical support, and the assurance that no situation is beyond God's redeeming power.

Biblical Perspective on Equality

What the Bible teaches about human dignity, justice, and our call to love all people equally.

The Apostles' Church affirms that all human beings are equal in worth and dignity before Jehovah God, having been created in His image (Genesis 1:26–27). Biblical equality is rooted in creation, redemption, and God's righteous character.

Equality in Creation

The Bible teaches that God created humanity, male and female, in His image. This establishes the inherent value of every person, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender, nationality, or social status (Genesis 1:27; Psalm 139:13–16).

Equality in Sin and Salvation

All people share a common condition of sin and a common need for salvation (Romans 3:23). Likewise, salvation in Jesus Christ is offered equally to all:

"There is neither Jew nor Greek, slave nor free, male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus."

— Galatians 3:28

Equality in the Church

The Church is the Ekklesia of God, composed of believers from every tribe, tongue, race, and nation. In Christ, all believers are members of one body, equally loved, gifted, and called to serve according to God's purpose (1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Revelation 7:9).

Equality and Responsibility

Biblical equality does not erase God-ordained roles, responsibilities, or moral standards. Rather, it calls believers to:

  • • Love one another sacrificially – Following Christ's example of self-giving love that values others above self (John 13:34–35).
  • • Reject discrimination, oppression, and injustice – Standing against all forms of prejudice and working for justice as God's representatives (Proverbs 31:8–9).
  • • Uphold holiness, truth, and righteousness – Maintaining biblical standards while extending grace, recognizing that true equality flows from God's character (Micah 6:8).

True equality is expressed through mutual respect, justice, compassion, and obedience to God's Word (Micah 6:8; James 2:1–9).

The Church's Witness

By living out biblical equality, the Church bears witness to the Kingdom of God and demonstrates the reconciling power of the Gospel in a divided world (John 17:21). Our unity in Christ becomes a powerful testimony to God's love for all people.

Living Out Biblical Equality

  • Welcome people from all backgrounds into fellowship
  • Address needs without partiality or favoritism
  • Speak against injustice in our communities
  • Cultivate diverse leadership that reflects God's family
  • Pray for and work toward reconciliation where divisions exist

The Apostles' Church—Who Are We?

We come from hundreds of ethnic and language backgrounds, yet we are united by common goals. Above all, we want to honor God, follow the teachings of Jesus Christ, and share His love with the world.

Our community is built on the foundation of apostolic teaching, sincere faith, and genuine fellowship. We believe in the transforming power of the Gospel and strive to live out our faith in practical, meaningful ways.
