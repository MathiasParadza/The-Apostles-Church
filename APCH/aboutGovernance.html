Church Governance

Understanding the biblical structure and leadership framework that guides The Apostles' Church

Divine Order in Leadership

Biblical governance for effective ministry and spiritual guidance

"For God is not a God of disorder but of peace—as in all the congregations of the Lord's people."
— 1 Corinthians 14:33

The Apostles' Church is organized and governed by councils composed of dedicated committees responsible for coordinating and synchronizing all areas of ministry. Our governance structure ensures both spiritual guidance and administrative efficiency, reflecting biblical principles of order and accountability.

Governance Structure

Two complementary systems working in harmony

Ecumenical Committees

Spiritual Guidance & Doctrine

Responsible for directing all ecumenical activities including doctrinal teaching and guardianship of the Church. These committees ensure the purity of doctrine and spiritual integrity of all church activities.

Doctrinal Committee

Oversees biblical teaching and ensures doctrinal purity

Worship Committee

Coordinates liturgical practices and worship services

Discipleship Committee

Oversees spiritual formation and Christian education

Membership: All clergy belong to ecumenical committees, with administration officers also eligible for appointment.

Administration Committees

Management & Operations

Oversees all administrative and management functions in the Church. These committees ensure efficient operations, financial stewardship, and proper resource management.

Finance Committee

Manages church finances and budgeting

Facilities Committee

Oversees church property and maintenance

Personnel Committee

Handles staff management and human resources

Membership: All administration officers belong to administration committees, with clergy also eligible for appointment to ensure integrated leadership.

Council Hierarchy

Three levels of governance working in unity

Branch Council
Local Congregation Leadership
Made up of all leaders of the congregation chaired by the Pastor and deputised by the Evangelist. Oversees the congregation and coordinates all Branch Subcommittees.
Regional Council
Multi-Congregation Oversight
Composed of all regional leaders led by an elected Executive Committee. Receives reports from all Regional Subcommittees and submits consolidated reports to the Synod.
Synod
Highest Governing Council
The APCH's highest governing council on earth, representing the authority of the General Conference. Consists of the Synod Executive and all Regional Bishops.
Synod Executive
Supreme Governing Body
Headed by the General Overseer, represents the Synod and General Conference when not in session. Provides continuous leadership and decision-making authority.
