Church Organizational Structure

Understanding the biblical and efficient structure that enables effective ministry across all levels of The Apostles' Church.

The Apostles' Church is organized into four distinct administrative levels, each designed for effective ministry, leadership, and spiritual growth.

Preaching Point

Satellite Assembly Point
The smallest assembly unit of The Apostles' Church, operating as a satellite point for the nearest branch. These are strategic locations for outreach and community engagement.
Leadership: Appointed Teacher assisted by Deacon, Secretary, and Treasurer
Purpose: Local outreach and foundational discipleship

Branch

Foundation of APCH
The foundational unit of The Apostles' Church, consisting of a congregation with established leadership and regular worship services. Each branch serves as a spiritual home for members in a specific locality.
Membership: 40-144 baptized members
Leadership: Pastor with appointed elders
Growth Strategy: Creates preaching points when exceeding 144 members

Region

Geographical Administration
A defined geographical area containing multiple branches, configured and recommended by the Secretariat and approved by the Synod Executive. Regions provide oversight and coordination for ministry across larger areas.
Requirements: Minimum 1,000 baptized members
Governance: Regional leadership council
Purpose: Strategic planning and resource coordination

The General Conference

Supreme Governing Body
The highest governing authority of The Apostles' Church, representing the entire membership. The General Conference makes key decisions, provides spiritual direction, and serves the Lord's Table during its meetings.
Meetings: Quarterly (April, July, October)
Authority: Supreme decision-making body
Biblical Basis: Deuteronomy 16 seasonal gatherings

General Conference Schedule

Following the biblical pattern established in Deuteronomy 16, The Apostles' Church holds three General Conferences each year to celebrate, make decisions, and serve the Lord's Table.

April
First Quarter Conference
Spring gathering focusing on spiritual renewal, strategic planning for the year, and celebrating new beginnings in Christ.
"Observe the month of Aviv and celebrate the Passover of the LORD your God" - Deuteronomy 16:1
July
Second Quarter Conference
Mid-year assessment, leadership development, and celebration of harvest blessings in ministry and spiritual growth.
"Celebrate the Festival of Weeks to the LORD your God" - Deuteronomy 16:10
October
Third Quarter Conference
Year-end gathering for thanksgiving, reflection on accomplishments, and preparation for the coming year's ministry.
"Celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles for seven days" - Deuteronomy 16:13
