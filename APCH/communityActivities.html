Church Activities

Join us in faith-building activities that strengthen our community and deepen our relationship with God

Building Our Faith Community

Engage in activities that nurture spiritual growth and fellowship

"And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another—and all the more as you see the Day approaching."
— Hebrews 10:24-25

The Apostles' Church Community involves believers in knowledge sharing and faith-building activities that strengthen our walk with God and build meaningful relationships within our spiritual family.

Holy Convocation

Divine Gathering & Worship

A holy fellowship of Yahweh with the saints. It involves prayers, the sharing of psalms, and study of the Word of Yahweh. The saints come into Yahweh's presence in this manner thrice a week on the fourth, fifth and eighth day.

This is the day when Yahweh gave light to the dark heavens and earth and separated light from the darkness.
Genesis 1:3-4

The Eighth or First Day of the week is the Main Divine Service during which the saints come with offerings and tithes as well. This is the day that the redemption of the Israelites from their bondage which mirrors the redemption of the whole world through Yehoshua HaMeshaich happened.

John 3:16

Solemn Feasts

Annual Spiritual Conferences

Seven days General Conference held thrice a year in April, July and October with neither the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth which is Yehoshua HaMeshiach.

For Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed. Therefore let us keep the Festival, not with the old bread leavened with malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
1 Corinthians 5:7-8

The LORD's Supper is conducted during each Solemn Feast, commemorating Christ's sacrifice and renewing our covenant commitment.

Hossanah

Daily Worship & Thanksgiving

Conducted every morning, afternoon and evening of each day of the week to dedicate and give thanks for the day and night to Yahweh. The morning and evening sessions are done by each group of neighbouring households while the afternoon session is conducted by individuals wherever they may be.

From the rising of the sun to the place where it sets, the name of the LORD is to be praised.
Psalm 113:3

Prayer Retreats

Sacred Times of Deep Communion

Traditionally, prayer retreats are held every Friday and Tuesday. Special retreats are organised on request and directive from Yahweh. These prayers take place at retreat centres called Rushanga, the Sacred Place and/or Holy Mountain.

But when you pray, go into your room, close the door and pray to your Father, who is unseen. Then your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.
Matthew 6:6

Bible Study

Systematic Scriptural Exploration

Indepth systematic exploration of sacred scriptures to build and equip believers with effective and efficient understanding and application of the Word of Yahweh.

All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.
2 Timothy 3:16

Community Outreach

Evangelism & Church Planting

Home visitation conducted by congregation leadership and reaching out to the world with the gospel of the Kingdom of Yahweh and planting churches by the team of evangelists and preachers.

Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.
Matthew 28:19

Family Ministry

CHINAI Fellowships

The Apostles Church conducts weekly gatherings, seminars and other activities that inculcate and uphold values and principles from the Biblical Worldview on marriage, parenting and family life through CHINAI (which means the fifth element) Fellowships for the Women, Men, Couples and Youth.

Unless the LORD builds the house, the builders labor in vain. Unless the LORD watches over the city, the guards stand watch in vain.
Psalm 127:1
