About The Apostles' Church

Discover our mission, leadership, and the divine purpose that guides our ministry

Our Divine Mandate

A Church Built on Apostolic Foundations

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you."
— Matthew 28:19-20

The Apostles' Church (APCH) is a vibrant Christian community committed to fulfilling the Great Commission through faithful ministry, sound doctrine, and compassionate service. Founded on apostolic principles, we strive to build a church that reflects God's kingdom on earth.

The Secretariat

Central Management Committee & Administrative Heart

The APCH Secretariat serves as the Central Management Committee and administrative organ that oversees the day-to-day activities of the Church from the Head Office. As the operational hub of our ministry, the Secretariat ensures smooth coordination, efficient management, and faithful stewardship of all church resources.

Comprised of dedicated volunteers, full-time employees, and part-time staff, the Secretariat represents a diverse team united in service to God's work. This includes key leadership positions such as the General Secretary, General Treasurer, Chairpersons of Fellowships, and auxiliary staff as determined by the Synod Executive to meet the evolving needs of our growing ministry.

Secretariat Structure

Key Components of Our Administrative Leadership

General Secretary

Chief administrative officer responsible for overall coordination of church operations, record-keeping, and implementation of Synod Executive decisions.

General Treasurer

Oversees all financial matters including budgeting, accounting, stewardship of resources, and financial reporting to ensure transparency and integrity.

Fellowship Chairpersons

Leaders of various church fellowships who coordinate specialized ministries and ensure effective pastoral care across different demographic groups.

Auxiliary Staff

Dedicated support personnel including administrative assistants, technical staff, and ministry coordinators who ensure smooth day-to-day operations.

Our Core Values

Principles That Guide Our Ministry

Biblical Authority

We submit to the absolute authority of Scripture as God's inspired Word and final authority in all matters of faith and practice.

Prayerful Dependence

We maintain constant communion with God through prayer, recognizing our complete dependence on His guidance and power.

Compassionate Service

We demonstrate Christ's love through practical service, caring for the spiritual, emotional, and physical needs of all people.

Integrity & Accountability

We uphold the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and accountability in all our relationships and operations.
