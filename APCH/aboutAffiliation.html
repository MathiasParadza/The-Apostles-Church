Our Affiliation & Governance

Understanding our autonomous governance while maintaining strategic partnerships for kingdom advancement.

Our Affiliation Statement

The Apostles' Church maintains a balanced approach to ecclesiastical relationships—exercising our God-given autonomy while engaging in meaningful partnerships that advance the Gospel and strengthen the global body of Christ.

This Apostles' Church is autonomous and maintains the right to govern its own affairs, independent of any denominational and/or religious sectarian control.

While we cherish our autonomy, we recognize the biblical principle of unity among believers. We retain the right and freedom to associate ourselves with movements, councils, and associations of churches whose mission aligns with our commitment to biblical truth and Gospel advancement.

Such associations are entered into only when, in the discretion of our Synod, they are considered essential to the furtherance of the aim and objects of the Holy Bible. We equally retain the right to terminate any association should it, by the standards of our Church, be found wanting in doctrinal integrity or missional alignment.

Our Principles of Autonomous Governance

Our autonomy is exercised under the following biblical principles that guide our governance and decision-making:

Biblical Authority

Final authority in all matters rests with Scripture alone, not external denominational structures.

Local Governance

Each congregation is led by locally-appointed elders under Christ's headship.

Doctrinal Integrity

We maintain freedom to teach and practice biblical truth without compromise.

Strategic Partnership

We voluntarily partner with like-minded ministries for greater Gospel impact.

"Christ is the head of the church, his body, of which he is the Savior."
— Ephesians 5:23

Our governance structure reflects our conviction that Christ alone is the head of His Church. All authority, decisions, and partnerships are submitted to His lordship and guided by His Word.
