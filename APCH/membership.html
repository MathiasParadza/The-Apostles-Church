Church Membership

Description

Membership in The Apostles' Church consists of all persons who have met the requirements for membership, having been approved by the congregation and are listed on the membership role. We maintain separate registers for different stages of spiritual journey:

Children's Register

Children consecrated in the church are listed here until age 18. Each child receives a Certificate of Consecration.

Baptism Register

All baptized members receive a Certificate of Baptism and are formally recorded in our membership database.

Active Membership

Fully committed members who participate in church life, voting, and leadership roles as appropriate.

Note: Children reaching age 18 without meeting full membership requirements are removed from the children's register and treated as visitors until they complete membership qualifications.

Qualifications for Membership

To become a full member of The Apostles' Church, individuals must meet the following requirements:

  • 1
    A personal commitment of faith in Jesus Christ for salvation
  • 2
    Baptism by triune immersion as a testimony of salvation
  • 3
    Registered at any church branch of The Apostles' Church
  • 4
    Completion of foundational lessons on church doctrines, aims, objectives, and Statement of Faith
  • 5
    Consecration and dedication of children to Jehovah God (for child membership)
  • 6
    Adoption from another Church whose basis of faith conforms with ours

Membership Transfer

From Another APCH Branch

Members moving between our congregations require a transfer letter detailing their spiritual standing, leadership involvement, and fulfillment of responsibilities. The new branch enlists them while removing them from their previous branch's active roll.

From a Compatible Church

Individuals from churches with compatible doctrines can join with a recommendation letter confirming good standing and acceptance at the Lord's Table. Additional instruction and confirmation of acceptance of our Statement of Faith may be required.

From an Incompatible Church

Those coming from churches with doctrinal differences must be baptized in The Apostles' Church to be accepted as members, regardless of previous baptism.

Members' Rights & Responsibilities

Consistent with our Constitution, every member enjoys specific rights and carries important responsibilities:

Participation Rights

Every active member has the right to participate in all church matters including financial affairs and administration.

Worship Commitment

Responsibility to attend divine services, worship gatherings, and the Lord's Table regularly.

Voting Privileges

Each member has full voting rights in business meetings and elections (one vote per member, no proxy voting).

Financial Support

Support the church through tithes, offerings, donations, and honoring financial commitments.

Spiritual Growth

Take full advantage of teaching programs and opportunities for spiritual development.

Service Commitment

Carry out duties assigned by church leadership to advance our biblical aims and objectives.

Membership Management

Exclusion from Membership

Members may be removed from the church roll for:

  • Gross indiscipline
  • Transfer to a different church
  • Apostasy (abandonment of faith)
  • Death

Restoration of Members

Former members can be restored by vote of the assembly from which they were dismissed or unanimous agreement of church officers, following the biblical principle of restoration found in:

"Now instead, you ought to forgive and comfort him, so that he will not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. I urge you, therefore, to reaffirm your love for him." — 2 Corinthians 2:5-11
