Giving & Stewardship

Partner with us in ministry through your faithful giving and support our mission to spread God's Word throughout our community and beyond.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

Biblical Principles of Giving

An Act of Worship

Giving is an expression of gratitude and worship to God, acknowledging that everything we have comes from Him. It's a tangible way to honor God with our resources.

Supporting Ministry

Your giving enables us to maintain our facilities, support missionaries, fund outreach programs, and provide resources for spiritual growth in our community.

Building Community

Through collective giving, we can support those in need, provide food and shelter, and create opportunities for fellowship and spiritual development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my online giving secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-compliant payment processing. Your financial information is never stored on our servers.

Will I receive a giving statement for tax purposes?

Yes, we provide quarterly giving statements and an annual statement in January for the previous year's tax deductions. You can also access your giving history through your online account.

Can I schedule recurring donations?

Yes, you can set up recurring donations weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly through our online giving platform. You can modify or cancel recurring donations at any time.

What percentage goes to administrative costs?

Less than 5% of donations cover payment processing fees and administrative costs. 95% of every dollar goes directly to ministry work.

Can I give stocks or other assets?

Yes, we accept stock donations and other non-cash gifts. Please contact our finance office at (555) 123-4567 ext. 202 for assistance with asset donations.
