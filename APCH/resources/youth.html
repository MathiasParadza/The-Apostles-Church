Youth & Young Adults

Young people are vital to the life and future of the Church. God calls them to live with purpose, faith, and courage.

Identity in Christ

True identity is found in being created and redeemed by God. Young people are valued, loved, and called according to God's purpose (1 Peter 2:9).

Faith in a Changing World

The Bible provides guidance for navigating modern challenges such as peer pressure, media influence, and moral decision-making (Romans 12:2).

Common Challenges:

Peer Pressure

Standing firm in faith among friends and classmates

Social Media

Navigating digital influence and maintaining authenticity

Career Choices

Finding God's direction for your future

Relationships

Building godly friendships and dating relationships

Discovering Purpose and Calling

God equips young believers with spiritual gifts and talents to serve Him and others. Every calling matters in God's Kingdom (1 Timothy 4:12).

Education, Work, and Integrity

Faith in God shapes attitudes towards education, work, and leadership. Believers are called to live with honesty, diligence, and excellence (Colossians 3:23).

Community and Belonging

The Church provides a safe and supportive community where young people can grow spiritually and build meaningful relationships that last a lifetime.
