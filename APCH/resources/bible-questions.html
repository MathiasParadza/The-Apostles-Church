Bible Questions Answered

The Bible addresses the deepest questions of life, faith, purpose, and eternity. At The Apostles' Church, we believe the Holy Scriptures are the inspired and authoritative Word of Jehovah God and provide trustworthy answers for every generation.

Who Is God?

God is the eternal Creator of heaven and earth. He is holy, loving, just, and faithful. The Bible reveals one true God who desires a relationship with humanity and who governs all creation with wisdom and compassion (Genesis 1:1; Psalm 145:8–9).

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

— Genesis 1:1

Who Is Jesus Christ?

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God who became human without ceasing to be divine. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for the forgiveness of sins, rose bodily from the dead, and ascended into heaven. He will return again in glory as Lord and King (John 1:1–14; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4).

What Is Salvation?

Salvation is God's free gift of eternal life, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. It brings forgiveness of sins, reconciliation with God, and transformation of life. Salvation cannot be earned by human effort but is received through repentance and faith (Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 10:9–10).

Why Does God Allow Suffering?

The Bible teaches that suffering entered the world through sin, yet God remains present and active in human pain. He uses suffering to draw people to Himself, to shape character, and to reveal His glory. In Christ, suffering is not the end; hope and restoration are promised (Romans 8:18; John 16:33).

What Happens After Death?

Death does not end existence. The Bible teaches a bodily resurrection and a final judgement. Those who belong to Christ will enjoy eternal life with God, while those who reject Him face eternal separation (1 Corinthians 15; Revelation 21:1–8).
