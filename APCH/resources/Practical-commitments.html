The Apostles' Church: Who Are We

Our practical commitments flow from our faith in Jesus Christ and our desire to live according to His Word, guided by the apostles' teaching and the leading of the Holy Spirit.

The Name

We commit to the name Apostles as given by the Lord Jesus Christ Himself, who is the Chief Apostle (Luke 6:12–13; Hebrews 3:1). In His divine purpose, He called and named His disciples apostles, setting them apart as a chosen people to seek first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness (Matthew 6:33). This calling fulfils the prophetic word spoken by Isaiah, that the Gentiles would see God's righteousness and that His servants would be called by a new name, given by the mouth of the LORD (Isaiah 62:2; 65:15; John 17:6–12).

The Lord Jesus Christ identifies Himself as the One sent by the Father and teaches that whoever receives Him, listens to Him, and submits to Him must also receive and honour the Father who sent Him (Matthew 10:40; Mark 9:37; Luke 10:16; John 5:23), to whom He returned after completing His work (John 16:5). In the fullness of His wisdom, Christ declared that He would send His servants into the world as apostles and prophets (Luke 11:49).

By naming His disciples apostles, Jesus Christ united them with Himself through His name and extended this unity to all who would believe through their testimony (John 17:20–23). On the Day of Pentecost, as the apostles preached the gospel of Jesus Christ, many believed and were added to them (Acts 2:37–44). We therefore affirm that no person has the authority to give another name to the Body of Christ, for salvation and identity are found in no other name but that of the Lord Jesus Christ (Acts 4:10–12).

"And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers."

— Acts 2:42

Sanctity of Marriage

We uphold the sanctity of marriage as a lifelong covenant between one man and one woman, which is not to be separated except by death. Accordingly, a person living with a spouse other than their first spouse while the former spouse is still living, whether through polygamy or divorce and remarriage, does not qualify for church leadership.

An exception applies only where such remarriage occurred before the individual became a baptised member of the APCH. Likewise, a person who marries a divorcee whose former spouse is still living does not qualify for leadership, except where the marriage took place prior to baptism into the APCH (Matthew 19:7–9; Mark 10:11; Luke 16:18; 1 Corinthians 7:2, 10–11).

Holy Convocation

We commit to holy fellowship with the saints through prayer, the sharing of psalms, and the study of the Word of God on the eighth day, also known as the first day of the week, which is observed as the Church's Main Divine Service. On this day, believers gather with offerings and tithes.

This is the day on which God first commanded light to shine and separated light from darkness (Genesis 1:3–4), and on which the redemption of Israel, foreshadowing the redemption of the world through the Lord Jesus Christ, was instituted (John 3:16; Exodus 12:16). It is also the day when the resurrection of the Lord Jesus Christ was first proclaimed and He was worshipped (Matthew 28:16).

The early Church met on this day to expound the Scriptures, break bread, and collect offerings (Acts 20:7; 1 Corinthians 16:1). Scripture identifies this day as the Lord's Day (Revelation 1:10), and God commanded it to be observed as a holy convocation (Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7; Numbers 29:1).

Solemn Feasts

We commit to holding General Conferences three times each year in April, July, and October observing them not with the leaven of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth, which is Jesus Christ (1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Leviticus 23).

Head Covering

We adhere to the scriptural instruction that women are to cover their heads during worship, in obedience to the order and teaching set forth in the Scriptures (1 Corinthians 11:14–15).

Modest Appearance

We commit to dressing in accordance with New Testament teaching, maintaining conformity to Scripture concerning outward adornment. This includes restraint in the use of jewellery and cosmetics that create an unnatural appearance, so that conduct and appearance reflect holiness and devotion to God (1 John 2:15–16; 1 Peter 3:3–5; 1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:1–2).

Total Abstinence

We commit to total abstinence from the following:

  • Unclean foods and blood Acts 15:28–29; Romans 14:2–3, 17; 1 Corinthians 8:8; 1 Timothy 4:1–5
  • All liquor and strong drink Proverbs 20:1; 23:29–32; Isaiah 28:7; 1 Corinthians 5:11; 6:10
  • The use of tobacco in any form, opium, morphine, and other harmful drugs Isaiah 55:2; 1 Corinthians 10:31–32; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Ephesians 5:3–8; James 1:21
  • Ungodly amusements, social outings, dances, and media entertainment that contradict biblical holiness 1 John 2:15–16; Romans 13:14; 6:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; Philippians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 6:14, 19–20; 7:1; 1 Timothy 2:9; 2 Peter 1:4; Galatians 5:19

Biblical Foundation

"Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God."

— 2 Corinthians 7:1

Our Commitment to Holiness

These practical commitments reflect our desire to live in obedience to God's Word, to honor Jesus Christ in all things, and to be a people set apart for His purposes. We invite all who share these convictions to join us in this journey of faith.

"But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light." — 1 Peter 2:9
