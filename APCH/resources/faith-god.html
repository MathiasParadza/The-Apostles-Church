Faith in God

Faith is central to the Christian life. It is through faith that believers receive salvation and learn to live in daily dependence on God.

What Is Faith?

Faith is confident trust in God's character, promises, and Word. It involves believing in God's truth and acting in obedience, even when circumstances are unclear (Hebrews 11:1, 6).

Faith and Salvation

Salvation is received by faith in Jesus Christ. This faith leads to repentance, transformation, and a lifelong journey of following Christ (Romans 3:22–24).

Growing in Faith

Faith grows through prayer, reading the Bible, worship, fellowship, and obedience. As believers walk with God, their trust in Him deepens (Romans 10:17).

Ways to Grow in Faith:

Daily Devotion

Regular prayer and Bible study strengthen spiritual foundations.

Christian Fellowship

Worship and community with believers encourage and build faith.

Obedience

Putting faith into action through daily obedience to God's Word.

Service

Using spiritual gifts to serve others and demonstrate faith.

Faith in Difficult Times

Trials test and refine faith. The Bible encourages believers to persevere, knowing that God is faithful and works all things for good (James 1:2–4; Romans 8:28).

Faith Expressed Through Action

True faith produces good works. While works do not save, they are evidence of a living and active faith (James 2:17).

"Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see."
— Hebrews 11:1
