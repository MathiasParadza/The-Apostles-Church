Marriage & Family

Marriage and family are God's gift to humanity and are central to His design for society and spiritual growth.

God's Design for Marriage

Marriage is a covenant relationship between one man and one woman, instituted by God and intended to reflect His love, faithfulness, and unity (Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6).

Love and Mutual Respect

The Bible teaches mutual love, respect, and submission within marriage. Husbands and wives are called to serve one another in Christlike love (Ephesians 5:21–33).

"Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ."

— Ephesians 5:21

Raising Godly Children

Parents are entrusted with nurturing children in love, discipline, and the knowledge of God. The Church supports families in this sacred responsibility (Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Proverbs 22:6).

  • Model Christlike character and values
  • Teach God's Word through daily living
  • Establish regular family worship
  • Pray together as a family
  • Extend grace while maintaining discipline

Healing and Forgiveness in Families

All families face challenges. Through forgiveness, prayer, and biblical guidance, broken relationships can be restored and strengthened (Colossians 3:13–14).

The Family and the Church

The Church is a spiritual family where individuals and households receive encouragement, accountability, and care. Together, we grow in faith and support one another through every season of life.
