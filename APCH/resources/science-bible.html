Science & the Bible

The Bible and science address different questions but are not in conflict. Scripture reveals who created the universe and why, while science explores how creation functions.

God the Creator

The Bible affirms God as the Creator and Sustainer of all things. Creation reflects His wisdom, order, and power (Genesis 1; Psalm 19:1).

"The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands."

— Psalm 19:1

The Bible and Scientific Inquiry

The Bible is not a science textbook but speaks truthfully about reality. Scientific discovery, when rightly understood, can deepen appreciation for God's creation.

Human Life and Dignity

Human beings are created in God's image, giving every person inherent worth and dignity. This belief shapes Christian ethics and respect for life (Genesis 1:26–27).

Faith and Reason

Christian faith encourages thoughtful reflection and does not reject reason. Believers are called to love God with heart, soul, and mind (Matthew 22:37).

"All truth is God's truth, wherever it may be found."

— Christian maxim

Ethics and Responsibility

As science and technology advance, biblical principles guide responsible use, compassion, and respect for human life and the environment.

  • Stewardship of creation (Genesis 2:15)
  • Respect for human dignity at all stages of life
  • Ethical considerations in medical research
  • Responsible technological development
  • Environmental conservation as worship

Areas of Harmony

Cosmic Order

The universe's precise physical constants align with biblical descriptions of God's orderly creation.

Biological Complexity

Intricate design in living organisms reflects divine craftsmanship.

Moral Consciousness

Human moral awareness corresponds to being made in God's image.

Recommended Reading

  • "The Language of God" by Francis Collins
  • "The Case for a Creator" by Lee Strobel
  • "Science and Faith" by C. John Collins
  • "God's Undertaker" by John Lennox
