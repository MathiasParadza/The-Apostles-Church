The Meaning of Our Name

"You shall be called by a new name that the mouth of the LORD will bestow." - Isaiah 62:2

Why "Apostles"?

Apostles is the new name that Yahweh gave to His chosen people who seek His Kingdom and His righteousness (Luke 6:13; Matthew 6:33). This prophetic name was foretold by Isaiah:

And the Gentiles shall see thy righteousness, and all kings thy glory: and thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of the LORD shall name.
— Isaiah 62:2

Yahweh called us by this new name to save us from the curse of the old name:

And ye shall leave your name for a curse unto my chosen: for the LORD Yahweh shall slay thee, and call his servants by another name.
— Isaiah 65:15

Biblical Significance of Our Name

A Chosen People

As Yahweh's servants and chosen people, we are a new tribe and holy nation, a royal priesthood of His Kingdom.

New Covenant

Founded on a new covenant written not on stone tablets but on the tablets of our hearts (Jeremiah 31:33).

Divine Union

We are united through the blood and body of Yehoshua HaMeshiach, becoming one family in Him.

Our Apostolic Identity

When we partake of the divine through communion, our lips are touched by Yahweh's power, removing our iniquity and atoning for our sins (Isaiah 6:6-7). In this spiritual nourishment, we undergo an Ezekielian experience:

Spiritual Nourishment

Eating the scroll of God's Word, making it sweet as honey in our mouths (Ezekiel 3:1-3).

Global Commission

Commissioned to speak to all creation, not just those of familiar speech (Mark 16:15-18).

Divine Tongues

Speaking through the Holy Spirit in various tongues as at Pentecost (Acts 2:3-4).

The Apostolic Ministries

The ultimate goal of the Gospel is to unite all peoples into one tribe or nation of one faith and knowledge of Yahweh through Yehoshua HaMeshiach. This tribal nation is the body that is perfected and edified by its founding father, Yehoshua, who is the Chief Apostle and high priest of the order of Melchizedek (Hebrews 3:1).

Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus.
— Hebrews 3:1

As citizens of this holy nation, we are called apostles—a royal priesthood and vocation just as our father who is the Chief Apostle. The founding of this tribal nation is prophetic, and our ministries reflect this apostolic calling:

Apostolic Prophets

Proclaiming the culture of our tribal nation through prophetic utterance.

Apostolic Evangelists

Baptizing those who believe in the name of our Chief Apostle.

Apostolic Pastors

Shepherding and nurturing believers to grow in faith and maturity.

Apostolic Teachers

Instructing through study and meditation on the Gospel of our nation.

Key Principle: Before one can prophesy, evangelize, shepherd, or teach, one must first acquire the citizenship—to be an apostle. One must be sent, possessing the authority. If not an apostle, then in whose name and by what authority shall one minister?

Unity in the Apostolic Name

When Yehoshua named His disciples apostles (Luke 6:12-13), He reconciled and united Himself with them through this name. His prayer in John 17 reveals His desire for unity—that all believers would be one with Him and with each other.

And when it was day, he called unto him his disciples: and of them he chose twelve, whom also he named apostles.
— Luke 6:13

There is only one Ekklesia (Church), and it bears the name that Yehoshua commanded: The Apostles. No other name has been given by Yehoshua to His disciples. Therefore, no one has the right to change or give another name to the Body of Christ:

Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.
— Acts 4:12

On the Day of Pentecost, when three thousand people believed and were baptized, they were added to the Apostles and continued steadfastly in the apostles' doctrine (Acts 2:41-42). As Apostle Paul reminds us:

Therefore you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens of the saints and members of Yahweh's household, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus Himself as the cornerstone.
— Ephesians 2:19-20

We, therefore, as the Ekklesia of Yahweh, are The Apostles and uphold the teachings of the Holy Bible, maintaining the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.
