ABOUT THE APCH

Secretariat

The APCH Secretariat is its Central Management Committee and administrative organ that runs the everyday activities of the Church as the Head Office. The Secretariat is made up of voluntary, full and part time employees of the Church including the General Secretary, General Treasurer, Chairpersons of Fellowships and auxiliary staff as are determined from time to time by the Synod Executive.
