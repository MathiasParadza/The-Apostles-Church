THE NAME

Apostles is a new name that Yahweh gave to us His chosen people that seek His Kingdom and His righteousness (Luke 6:‬13; Matthew 6:33)). This was prophesied by Prophet Isaiah (62:2) saying:

And the Gentiles shall see thy righteousness, and all kings thy glory:and thou shalt be called by a new name,which the mouth of the LORD shall name.

Yahweh called us by this new name to save us from the curse of the old name:

And ye shall leave your name for a curse unto my chosen: for the LORD Yahweh shall slay thee, and call his servants by another name (Isaiah 65:15).

Being His servants and chosen people, we are a new tribe and holy nation, a priesthood of His Kingdom as 1 Peter 2:9 explains:

But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for Yahweh’s own possession, to proclaim the virtues of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.

As a new tribe and holy nation, we are founded on a new covenant written not on stone tablets but the tablets of our innermost parts, that is, our heart, soul, mind and strength as Jeremiah 31:33 prophesied:

But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and I will be their Yahweh, and they shall be my people.

Given that this new covenant is written on the tablets of our heart and not stones, it is rooted in flesh and blood. We, as clanswomen and men of this new tribe, citizens of this holy nation and Kingdom, are related with each other and the LORD our Yahweh and King by this flesh and blood but not the pound of our own flesh and blood coursing through our veins but by the blood we drink and flesh we eat of the LORD Yehoshua HaMeshiach as Mark 14:22‭-‬24 explains:

And as they did eat, Jesus took bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take, eat: this is my body. And he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave it to them: and they all drank of it. And he said unto them, This is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many.

Now, when we eat and drink of the divine, our lips like that of Isaiah are touched by Yahweh’s power which removes our iniquity and atone for our sin:

Then one of the seraphim flew to me, and in his hand was a glowing coal that he had taken with tongs from the altar. And with it he touched my mouth and said: “Now that this has touched your lips, your iniquity is removed and your sin is atoned for” (Isaiah 6:6-7).

Further, in eating and drinking of the divine, we undergo an Ezekielian experience:

“Son of man,” He said to me, “eat what you find here. Eat this scroll, then go and speak to the house of Israel.” So I opened my mouth, and He fed me the scroll. “Son of man,” He said to me, “eat and fill your stomach with this scroll I am giving you.” So I ate, and it was as sweet as honey in my mouth. Then He said to me, “Son of man, go now to the house of Israel and speak My words to them. For you are not being sent to a people of unfamiliar speech or difficult language, but to the house of Israel – not to the many peoples of unfamiliar speech and difficult language whose words you cannot understand. Surely if I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you (Ezekiel 3:1-6).

This means that we become ready for the Great Commission as Yehoshua HaMeshiach commands in Mark 16:15-18:

And He said to them, “Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature. Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. And these signs will accompany those who believe: In My name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues; they will pick up snakes with their hands, and if they drink any deadly poison, it will not harm them; they will lay their hands on the sick, and they will be made well.

Unlike Jeremiah who speaks only to one house, the house of Israel that has a familiar and easy language, ourselves have been commissioned to speak the whole creation, the many peoples of unfamiliar speech and difficult language whose words by our own human experience we cannot understand. But these people listen to us and we understand their different difficult languages because first, we have Yahweh’s own tongue, and second, we speak their various languages by fire and the Spirit of Yahweh as shown in Acts 2:3‭-‬4:

And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them. And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

This is the ultimate goal of the gospel of Yehoshua HaMeshiach which Paul outlines in Ephesians 4: to Unite All Peoples into One Tribe or Nation of the One Faith and Knowledge of the One Yahweh through Yehoshua HaMeshaich who is the Yahweh incarnate in all righteousness. This tribal nation is the body that is perfect and edified by the work of its founding father, Yehoshua HaMeshiach. The citizens of this nation are called the apostles, a royal priesthood or vocation just as their father who is the Chief Apostle and high priest of the order of Melchizedek. The founding of this tribal nation is prophetic, and the founding father commissioned the citizens (Apostles) to proclaim (Prophets) the culture of this tribal nation baptising those who believe (Evangelists) in his name (apostle) and shepherding or nurturing them to grow (Pastors) through and by the study and meditating upon the gospel of this nation (Teachers). Therefore, what we have are Apostolic Prophets, Apostolic Evangelists, Apostolic Pastors and Apostolic Teachers.

This is how it was among the twelve apostles, there were these various gifts. Before you can prophesy, evangelise, shepherd or teach, you have to acquire the citizenship, to be a citizen, to be an apostle. You have to be sent, that is, to have the authority. If you are not an apostle, then how and in whose name and by what authority shall you prophesy, evangelise, shepherd or teach? Those who are sent (which is the meaning of apostles) by the One who was sent (which is the meaning of Yehoshua being the Chief Apostle) are known as Apostles. Yehoshua HaMeshiach is the Apostle as the scripture say in Hebrews 3:1:

Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus.

Throughout the gospels, Yehoshua HaMeshiach describes himself as such, a person who was sent, a messenger, the apostle of Yahweh. Accordingly, He emphasises that whoever sees him, receives him and listens to him, accepts and submits to the One who sent Him (Matthew 10:40; Mark 9:37; Luke 10:16; John 5:23). When He completed His works, Yehoshua said in John 16:5 that: “But now I am going to Him who sent Me”. To execute the Great Commission of a King, you have to and must be sent and be identified as such by the King that you are the sent one, the apostle.
That Yehoshuah HaMeshiach is an Apostle, by naming His disciples apostles He reconciled and united himself with these disciples through His name of Apostle as Luke 6:12‭-‬13 records:

And it came to pass in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray, and continued all night in prayer to Yahweh. And when it was day, he called unto him his disciples: and of them he chose twelve, whom also he named apostles.

When this principal prayer He prayed all night is examined as detailed in John 17, it shows that Yehoshua asked the Father to unite Him with the first disciples together with those who would believe in Him through these disciples. Therefore all those who believe in Yehoshua HaMeshiach become like Him in all things as there are no two or more Ekklesias, theEkklesia of the disciples and another one or others for those who would later believe. There is only one Ekklesia which is named as Yehoshua commanded, The Apostles.
There is no any other name that Yehoshua HaMeshiach ever gave His disciples beside this name, apostles. For this reason, there is no any person who has the right of changing or giving another name to the Body of Christ beside this name as Acts 4:10‭-‬12 puts it:

Be it known unto you all, and to all the people of Israel, that by the name of Yehoshua HaMeshiach of Nazareth, whom ye crucified, whom Yahweh raised from the dead, even by him doth this man stand here before you whole. This is the stone which was set at nought of you builders, which is become the head of the corner. Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.

When the twelve Apostles preached the gospel of Yehoshua on the Day of Pentecost, three thousand people believed and where baptized and became one with the Apostles. The Bible teaches that these three thousand were added to the Apostles and they started propounding the teaching of the Apostles as recorded in Acts 2:37-44:

Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do? Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Yehoshu HaMeshiach for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the LORD our Yahweh shall call. And with many other words did he testify and exhort, saying, Save yourselves from this untoward generation. Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls. And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers. And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles. And all that believed were together, and had all things common.

Accordingly, Apostle Paul continues to encourage us like he did to the believers at Ephesus in Ephesians 2:19-20 that we are no longer outsiders and foreigners but citizens of the Holy Nation, members of The Apostles Church which is he household of Yahweh with Yehoshua HaMeshiach as the Chief Apostle:

Therefore you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens of the saints and members of Yahweh’s household built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus Himself as the cornerstone.

We, therefore, as the Ekklesia of Yahweh, are The Apostles and uphold the teachings of the Holy Bible.
